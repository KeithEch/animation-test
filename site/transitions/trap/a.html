Can content get stranded?

What this tests: a page transition (T3, scripted fade) combined with IntersectionObserver fade-ins (fade-in B). Combinations like this are where content gets left invisible after navigating, most often on Back. The badge in the top-right corner checks for it automatically.

  1. Scroll about halfway down this page.
  2. Tap a "Go to Page B" link.
  3. Press Back, or swipe back on the iPhone.
  4. The badge must say PASS. Scroll further: newly revealed blocks must keep fading in.
  5. Repeat with Forward, and with reloading the page.
